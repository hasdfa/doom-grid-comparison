At 96 × 60, synthetic spans measured 12.7 and 12.3 FPS; direct roots measured 5.7 and 6.3. The original v10 spans measured 10.0 and 11.7 in this diagnostic session. Cell roots with added containment measured 5.4 and 5.2.

High-resolution gains were small and do not establish a speedup under shared-server variation. Version 12 restores the previously faster structure, keeps disabled selection/reordering and identity-safe ref cleanup, and leaves the other six adapters unchanged. These are browser/device-specific observations, not statistically established speedups. A single trial is insufficient for significance testing. Unrelated CPU-heavy jobs were observed on this shared server during measurement, so system contention is a material confounder. Values come from the app's rounded results. Mounting and warmup are excluded. A paused 96 × 60 TanStack gameplay grid remains mounted in both versions. Each test starts in a fresh browser, avoiding accumulated memory from previous renderer mounts.

Synthetic: 60 full-color frames after eight warmups, animation-frame paced. DOOM: fresh engine per renderer, one-second warmup, three-second walk, 100 ms Ctrl press every 500 ms. Live engines can sample different frames and changed-pixel counts. JavaScript update time excludes later browser painting; frame intervals do not prove presentation. Each renderer has a three-minute startup allowance and a five-minute measurement observation limit; missing values are not zero. Earlier Chrome 157 combined-suite attempts were interrupted by automation stalls and are excluded.
